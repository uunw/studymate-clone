# Product

## Register

product

## Users

KMITL undergraduates, primarily on mobile, often under registration-window time
pressure. They arrive to: look up a subject and read senior reviews before enrolling;
check how their transcript maps onto their curriculum's credit requirements; and plan
which sections to register for (clashes, eligibility, free electives). A smaller admin
audience (curriculum maintainers) manages the academic reference hierarchy
(faculties → departments → programs → curricula → groups). Interface language is Thai.

## Product Purpose

Open-source rebuild of KMITL StudyMate: subject reviews plus a curriculum/registration
tracker. It exists because the original (kmitl-savvy-students) went offline. Success is
a student trusting it enough to make a real enrollment decision from it — accurate
progress against their curriculum, honest peer reviews, and a registration plan that
correctly flags clashes and eligibility. It is not affiliated with KMITL.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy and academic. Calm, credible, registrar-grade — quietly authoritative data a
student can act on, not a flashy consumer app. Three words: dependable, clear, unfussy.
Thai-first voice; plain language over jargon. Confidence comes from legible hierarchy and
correct information, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS / AI slop.** No cream/sand body backgrounds, gradient text, hero-metric
  templates, identical icon-card grids, or glassmorphism. Stay distinctive and intentional.
- **Heavy institutional KMITL.** Must not feel like the official registrar portal: dated,
  dense officialdom, government-form density. This is the student-friendly alternative.
- **Over-designed / flashy.** No heavy animation, decorative excess, or spectacle. Students
  use this under deadline pressure; function leads.

## Design Principles

1. **Decisions you can trust.** Every screen supports a real academic decision; correctness
   and legibility of data outrank visual flourish.
2. **Mobile-first, registration-crunch-ready.** Designed for a phone during the registration
   window: fast, scannable, thumb-reachable, resilient on small viewports.
3. **Thai-first.** Thai is the primary language, not a translation layer. Typography, line
   length, and copy are tuned for Thai script (Noto Sans Thai).
4. **Quiet authority.** Restrained color and a single brand blue carry hierarchy; emphasis
   comes from weight, scale, and spacing rather than ornament.
5. **Honest states.** Empty, loading, error, and "not eligible / clashes" states are
   first-class — a tool students rely on must never look broken or ambiguous.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, visible keyboard focus, labelled
inputs, logical heading order, touch targets ≥ 44px. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
Thai script must remain legible at body sizes and on small screens.
